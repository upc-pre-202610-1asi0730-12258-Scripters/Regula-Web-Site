Frequently Asked Questions

Does Regula detect gas leaks in real time?
Yes. Regula continuously monitors gas cylinders and instantly alerts operators when abnormal gas leak patterns are detected.
What processes can I manage from the platform?
You can manage inventory, deliveries, customer records, routes and operational monitoring.
Is Regula suitable for small distributors?
Absolutely. Regula adapts to both small and large gas distribution operations.
Can I track delivery drivers in real time?
Yes. The platform provides live tracking and route monitoring for delivery drivers.
Can I register customers who buy on credit?
Yes. Regula allows customer credit management, payment tracking and debt monitoring.
